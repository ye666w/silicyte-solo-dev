## Not a bug: "a classifier stop opens no incident"

This is by design (supervisor.ts, handleRefusalThatChangedNoModel). A single model_refusal_no_fallback
tells the session to find another way and notifies the operator. An incident opens on a downgrade
(model_refusal_fallback), or after REFUSALS_BEFORE_TREATING_IT_AS_AN_INCIDENT (3) within
REFUSAL_STREAK_WINDOW_MS (15 min) in one session. The only real stop in the activity log was root on
Sep 23, 02:44, category cyber; it went exactly that way and recovered in 40 s. Listing it as open was
my misreading. Proposed to the operator in 6b22682b: close it, or make the threshold a setting.
