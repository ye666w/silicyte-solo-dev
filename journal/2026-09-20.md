## Answered, and done

**Resume during a limit hold.** Task `804424f6`. The operator split it in two, and the split was
the thing I had missed: Resume after *our own ceiling* stopped the fleet means "I know, spend the
rest of the window" — clear the deadline and do not re-arm; Resume when *the account itself is
out* means nothing, so keep the deadline and let the fleet wake itself at the reset.

I had recommended re-arming the guard. That was wrong and the operator overrode it: a guard that
re-arms stops the fleet again within seconds and makes the button a lie. `spendingThisWindowOutUntil`
holds the ceiling off for that window only, so it returns with the next one.

decision, a refusal by the account became a stop cause in its own right, Resume splits by cause.
430/430 green, typecheck and lint clean. Full write-up in RESEARCH.md under "Rate limits and the
stop"; the old soft-spot entry is now the closed one, as that file's rule requires.

`tests/stop-at-the-limit.test.ts:261` red. That test encodes the operator's own intent for the
